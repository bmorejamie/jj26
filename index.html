03InfoDenver, CO · 39.74° N, 104.99° W

It’s all UX to me.

I’m a senior UI/UX designer at Interactive Strategies, where I design websites, apps and interactive experiences for well-known national organizations and brands. I’m also product owner of our design system and lead our agentic design-to-code workflow.

Over the years I’ve designed everything from skateboards to agentic workflows — Thrasher ads, nonprofit websites, a mobile app for airline pilots, CTV spots. I love code for the same reason I love designing apps: I like to build things. Now, working with agents, I can design and build just about anything. It’s a magical time to be alive.

When I’m not building things with agents, I’m building things in the real world. During lockdown I built a Sprinter van, my escape vehicle to the mountains for me and my bikes. It’ll never be done. I also make drum and bass when I can find the time. No matter where I roam, my dog Summit is by my side.
