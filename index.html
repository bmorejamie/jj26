About

Eighteen years, never a straight line.

I'm Jamie — a designer of eighteen-some years, from the skateboard industry to global agencies to nonprofits doing work that matters. I live where design meets build, and lately that means shipping real things with AI.

I wear a lot of creative hats — motion is a favorite, I'll always love After Effects — but I'm most at home untangling complex UX problems and staying close to the build. I've always loved code almost as much as design, which is probably why working with AI agents feels less like a new trick and more like the tool I'd been waiting for.

Off the clock I'm on something with two wheels — motorcycle, road, mountain — usually going a little too fast.
