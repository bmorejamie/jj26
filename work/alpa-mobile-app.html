ALPA

A daily companion app for airline pilots. Track multileg commutes and jumpseat options with live delay status, monitor flight- and duty-time limits against federal regulations, and stay connected to union news, contract resources and emergency support. Dense, regulation-heavy information design, made to feel calm at five in the morning in a crew room.

Notes

Three screens carry the app. Saved Flights handles multileg itineraries as cards with swipe actions, delay and on-time status pills, gate information and jumpseat policy links. Cumulative Limitations renders flight-hour and duty-period limits as progress bars with time remaining. Home is a member feed: contract cards, union news, an emergency hotline.

Navy, blue and red brand palette on card-based utility UI, with a custom icon set in the tab bar and blueprint-style aircraft linework as background texture. The density is regulatory and unavoidable; hairline rules, progress bars and generous hierarchy are what keep it readable.
