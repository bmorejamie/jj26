Six motion studies for the mark — one easing curve, drawn different ways. All rendered in the medium it will ship in. Hover, click, and loiter; some of them notice.

C — Proofloop
The curve explaining itself: one dot climbs the graph as time passes, the dot below moves with the easing it produces. The mark is a working diagram.
D — Argumentmorph
Linear, then the browser default, then ours — the mark morphs through the wrong answers and settles on the right one.
E — Alivecursor
The curve bends toward your cursor and springs back when you leave — overshoot and settle, tuned like a good keyframe. A toy, and maybe a 404 page.
