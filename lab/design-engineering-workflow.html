Artifact · 004

Design engineering workflow

Collapse design / FE / requirements sprawl into one living, code-based artifact. Storybook (or the equivalent) as source of truth; designers as art directors of a shared system — not exporters of static piles.

Stub — outline only. Voice rewrite later.

The sprawl tax

Specs, mocks, tickets, and threads each hold a partial truth. Translation between them is where quality leaks — and where nobody owns the whole picture.

One living artifact

Put the contract in something that runs: states, tokens, acceptance criteria beside the component. The room everyone shares is the system, not a folder of exports.

Designers as art directors

Direction and judgment over pixel farming. Front-end as co-author of the same object. The craft shift is role clarity, not a new tool fad.

What changes in the handoff

Requirements ride with the component. Less “did you see the latest file?” — more “does this state match the living Spec?” Placeholder until the voice pass.

What this isn’t

Not a vendor pitch. Not a confidential company playbook. A craft thesis you can steal the shape of — method without the private details.
