Building Forge

Forge was my first agent. Where it all began.

I hadn’t opened the command line in over a decade when I decided to set up a Hermes Agent. Agents weren’t ubiquitous like they are now, and it was far from a one-click install. Just open-source setup, a couple of long, frustrating nights, and deciding what permissions to grant while everyone online was panicking about agents emptying their bank accounts.

48 hours later, it was up and running, and I knew one of the first things I wanted was a memory system that was robust and portable. I went the Obsidian vault route. I know, it’s ultimately just markdown files (but with that sick graph view), and it’s been game-changing for me. Those learnings carried straight into my Claude setup at work, and eventually into a whole team of agents at home.
