Building Forge

Standing up a hive agent is personality and identity work — not “another coding bot.” Forge (Hermes) owns personalization, voice, and guardian. This entry is how that lane becomes a distinct presence in the Buzz hive.

Stub — outline only. Voice rewrite later.

The wrong default

New agents show up as generic helpers. The lazy label is “coding bot.” That misses the point when the job is taste, tone, and watching the edges of a system.

The lane

Forge’s lane is personalization, voice, and guardian — not knowledge base, not coding hands. Clarity here keeps the hive from collapsing into interchangeable mouths.

Identity in the hive

One brain → one Buzz identity. Forge sits beside TopBot and Personal Claude as a named presence, not a second pair of coding hands with a different avatar.

Making it feel like someone

Soul rules, warmth, and what “guardian” means day to day. The craft is the personality file and the habits that keep it honest — details land on the voice pass.

What I’d show next

A lane card, a few public-safe voice samples, and a tight link back to the Slack-for-agents hive cast — once the writing is mine again.
