The Lab, as a lab

It's called the Lab. It should probably act like one.

These entries carry two dates, when I had the thought and when I wrote it up, so the order stays honest even when I post out of sequence. Reading that back, my mind wandered to the graph view in Obsidian. I've always loved those. What if these cards just floated in an interactive 3D graph instead of a linear timeline?

I wouldn't want it as the only view. The timeline stays. But I like the idea of the Lab always being a place to experiment with alternate views and UIs, and making it more dynamic.

I'm really trying to unthink things lately, in the age of agents. We've been restricted for so long on what we're able to actually build, and UI and UX has gotten very pattern oriented. Patterns are great. There's a reason they exist, and people expect things to happen a certain way. But the sense of adventure in anything other than a design site is kind of gone.

And I do wonder if my theory holds up: agents rebuild everything, websites become containers, and people start building UIs custom to them, the way they want to take in information. What a fascinating time. Scary as fuck, but might as well have fun along the way to wherever we're going.

I rambled about all this over dinner, caught myself not thinking in first principles, and told Claude to just build it while I ran errands. The graph reads the same entries as the timeline. Nothing is copied. It's another way in.

Switch the Lab to Graph and give it a spin.
