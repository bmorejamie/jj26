# ALPA Mobile App

**Status:** intake in progress — blurb drafted by Claude from screens, awaiting Jamie's corrections
**Sort:** most recent work (position 1 for now)

## Blurb (draft)

A daily-companion app for airline pilots: track multileg commutes and jumpseat options with
live delay status, monitor flight- and duty-time limits against federal regulations (FTDT),
and stay connected to union news, contract resources, and emergency support. Dense,
regulation-heavy information design made to feel calm at 5am in a crew room.

## Screens on hand (3, previews seen in chat — originals still to be uploaded)

1. **Saved Flights** — multileg itinerary cards (DEN→MIA, 3 segments), swipe actions
   (notify / delete), delay + on-time status pills, gate info, KCM and jumpseat-policy links.
2. **FTDT — Cumulative Limitations** — flight-hour and duty-period limits as progress bars
   with percentages and remaining time; Add Duty Period CTA; tabbed (Cumulative / Duty
   Periods / Archive).
3. **Home** — member feed: survey banner, personalized contract card ("Welcome Captain…"),
   MEC news cards, emergency hotline CTA, bottom nav (Home / Jumpseat / KCM / FTDT / My MEC).

## Design notes (Claude's read, for the eventual case study)

- Navy/blue/red brand palette; card-based utility UI; status pills for flight state.
- Custom icon set in the tab bar (jumpseat, pilot/KCM, duty-clock, MEC gavel-mic).
- Heavy information density (regulatory hour-tracking) kept legible with hairline rules,
  progress bars, and generous type hierarchy. Blueprint-style aircraft linework as subtle
  background texture.
